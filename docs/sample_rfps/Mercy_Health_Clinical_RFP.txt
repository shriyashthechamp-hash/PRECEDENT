SECTION 1: CORPORATE & VENDOR PROFILE
1.1 Describe your organization's corporate structure, years in commercial operation, and executive leadership credentials in healthcare technology.
1.2 Provide verifiable references for at least two enterprise hospital networks currently utilizing your platform in a production clinical environment.

SECTION 2: CLINICAL WORKFLOW & TECHNICAL CAPABILITIES
2.1 Detail how your document reasoning engine processes unstructured clinical notes, pathology reports, and HL7/FHIR feeds with verifiable audit provenance.
2.2 Describe your multi-region high availability architecture and specify your recovery time objective (RTO) and recovery point objective (RPO) for Tier-1 clinical processing.

SECTION 3: SECURITY, DATA GOVERNANCE & PRIVACY
3.1 Provide your current SOC2 Type II audit attestation and HIPAA Security Rule compliance documentation.
3.2 State your customer data retention policy post-contract termination and provide the verified timeline for certified destruction of all Protected Health Information (PHI).
3.3 Detail your data encryption standards for sensitive patient data both at rest and in transit across public and private networks.

SECTION 4: SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENTS & OPERATIONS
4.1 Detail your standard customer support hours, response SLA matrices, and escalation protocols for hospital clinical teams. Specifically clarify coverage for critical Priority 1 (P1) system disruptions.
4.2 Outline your planned maintenance windows and monthly availability uptime guarantees, including financial remedy credits for service interruptions.
